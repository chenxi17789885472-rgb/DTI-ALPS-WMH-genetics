# Real MRI image reuse audit

## Decision used in the manuscript

Only the axial T2-FLAIR/PWMH/DWMH example from Cai et al. (2022) was reused. The article is distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license, which permits adaptation and redistribution with attribution. The cropped image is identified in Figure 1B as an illustrative image rather than imaging from a participant in the present GWAS analyses. The figure legend provides the source citation, license name, and license URL.
